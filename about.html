Tentang Jakarta Film Summit

Satu panggung, seluruh ekosistem film.

Jakarta Film Summit dibangun untuk mempertemukan kreativitas dan modal — ruang di mana sineas, investor, dan pembuat kebijakan merancang masa depan perfilman Asia Tenggara bersama-sama.

01Positioning

JAKARTA FILM COMMISSION LOUNGE

The Indonesian film industry is growing rapidly, yet opportunities for creators and investors to connect remain limited. The Jakarta Film Summit serves as an annual bridge that brings the two parties closer together.
