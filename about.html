About Jakarta Film Summit

One stage, the entire film ecosystem.

The Jakarta Film Summit was established to bring together creativity and capital a space where filmmakers, investors, and policymakers collaboratively shape the future of Southeast Asian cinema.

01Positioning

JAKARTA FILM COMMISSION LOUNGE

The Indonesian film industry is growing rapidly, yet opportunities for creators and investors to connect remain limited. The Jakarta Film Summit serves as an annual bridge that brings the two parties closer together.
