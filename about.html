A Platform for Jakarta's Film Ecosystem

What Is Jakarta Film Summit?

Jakarta Film Summit brings together government, investors, producers, filmmakers, and industry leaders to discuss the development of Jakarta as a film hub.

JFS provides a space for policy dialogue, investment conversations, production solutions, government engagement, and business connections.

01Positioning

Why JFS? Jakarta sits at the centre.

Indonesia’s screen economy continues to develop across production, investment, technology, distribution, and creative industries.

JFS creates a platform for the people and institutions shaping that ecosystem to meet, exchange ideas, explore opportunities, and build connections.
