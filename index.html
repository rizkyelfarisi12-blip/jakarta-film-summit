01About JFS

A Platform for Jakarta's Film Ecosystem

Jakarta Film Summit is a platform for dialogue, investment, production, and industry connections.

The summit brings together government and policy makers, investors, producers, filmmakers, creative businesses, and ecosystem partners to discuss the development of Jakarta as a film hub and its role within Indonesia’s growing screen economy.
